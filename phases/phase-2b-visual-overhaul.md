**Why this task exists.** A review of the running app (`/cinemas/1/auditorium/1?showtime=1&mode=3d`, walk mode, one seat hovered, seven selected) found the 3D experience looks unfinished and the HUD is messy. Findings, from the screenshot:

1. **Large translucent yellow, blue and red boxes** are drawn around seats. These look like the invisible interaction proxies (or per-seat "state shells") rendered visibly and far bigger than the seats. They hide the seats and look like debug geometry. A red triangle/box also sticks out at the bottom-left.
2. **The screen is blown out** (flat white/grey) and the room is **very dark and flat**: seats are near-black silhouettes, there is little depth, no sense of materials or atmosphere.
3. **HUD collisions:** the right panel is cut off under the navbar, the left panel sits on top of the screen, the "Ask CineVerse" button overlaps the FPS counter, the FPS/GPU debug overlay is visible, the row labels ("A", "3") float as huge circled badges, and the whole thing is cluttered in first-person mode.
4. **Logic and text bugs visible in the UI:**
   - The legend counts add up to 107 (Available 90 + Held 8 + Selected 7 + Booked 2) for a 100-seat auditorium.
   - The right panel note says "Seat 01 is on right, Seat 10 on left", but the product rule is that **seat 01 is on the audience's LEFT** (public IDs are mirrored from the GLB node numbers; `seat_id` number = 11 − node number; see `ANTIGRAVITY_PROMPT.md`). Also, seat E06 shows "5.3° Right" screen offset, but with the mirrored mapping E06 sits at x = −1.2 and the screen centre is to its **left**. This suggests the 3D scene may still use the un-mirrored numbering while the 2D map uses the mirrored one. **Prove which is true and fix it so 2D and 3D agree.**
   - The wording "Sightline is not obstruction-guaranteed" is confusing.

## Part B — Remove the boxes and fix seat states (highest priority)
- Interaction proxies must be **invisible** (not rendered at all; `visible=false` or a layer that is raycast-only, never `opacity: 0.2` or wireframes) and sized to the real seat bounds from `seat_layout_v2.json`/`walk_nav_v2.json`. Proxies must never appear in screenshots, in any mode or quality tier.
- **No box-shaped state shells.** Show seat state on the actual seat silhouette:
  - Available: natural seat colour with a subtle category-coloured accent (small, e.g. an emissive strip or a thin rim light), not a tint of the whole seat.
  - Hover: soft outline/fresnel rim on the real seat mesh plus the small label.
  - Selected: clearly different (red accent + check icon above the seat), still readable from the back of the room.
  - Held by someone else: amber accent with a gentle pulse. Booked: desaturated/darkened seat with a small cross icon (not colour alone).
  - Implement using cloned materials per seat (or per-instance colour/emissive attributes) with emissive/rim effects; never separate large transparent meshes. Selected-seat outlines may use a post-process outline limited to selected/hovered seats only.
- A debug flag (`?debug=proxies`, dev builds only) may show proxy boxes; it is OFF by default and absent from production builds. Add a Playwright assertion through `window.__cineverse` that the number of visible proxy objects is 0 in normal mode, plus a screenshot test that no large uniformly-coloured translucent regions exist near seats.
- Find and remove the stray red triangle/box at the bottom-left. Explain the cause in the report (selected seat's own mesh clipped by the near plane? a stray shell? a helper?).
- Legend counts must always sum to the seat total (available + held + booked + selected = 100); selected seats are not also counted as available. Add a unit test.

## Part C — Environment and graphics overhaul
Target look: a premium IMAX-style multiplex. Deep charcoal and near-black walls with fabric acoustic panels, **rich red velvet seats with soft highlights**, warm wall sconces, glowing aisle LED strips, a dark patterned carpet with a visible warm path along the aisles, a clean gradient ceiling with soft downlights, light haze with a faint projector beam, and a **bright but not clipped** screen. Atmospheric, readable, not murky. The GLB has a single vertex-colour material, so do all of this at runtime without editing the GLB geometry.

1. **Diagnose the darkness first** (report findings): check how vertex colours are interpreted (`COLOR_0` is linear; wrong colour-space handling is a common cause of both dark and washed-out results), tone mapping, exposure, `outputColorSpace`, light intensities (three.js physical units), missing fill/hemisphere light, double-sided back faces, and whether seat vertex colours are actually very dark in the data. Fix the root cause.
2. **Materials by node-name group** (assign per group at load, keep original geometry): seats (fabric/leather: roughness 0.6–0.85, sheen if available, red base), carpet/floor (dark, subtle procedural pattern or noise normal on high tier, slight sheen), walls and acoustic panels (matte fabric), trims (satin metal), tiers (carpet), ceiling (matte dark), sconces/LEDs (emissive), screen frame (black matte), speakers (dark metal), rails (satin metal). Documented table in `docs/3d-materials.md`.
3. **Lighting rig:** filmic tone mapping (ACES or AgX), tuned exposure, a subtle environment from procedural light panels for reflections, low hemisphere fill, warm point/spot lights at sconces and ceiling lights (limit shadow-casting lights to at most 2), a cool screen-spill light that matches the screen content, rim lights so seats separate from the background, contact shadows under seats and tiers (fake AO) and SSAO or equivalent on high quality only, subtle bloom restricted to emissive parts, tiny vignette, optional light film grain off by default. "Lights up" mode readable and welcoming; "Show mode" dim and cinematic. Seat states stay clear in both.
4. **Fix the blown-out screen:** screen material emissive intensity and tone mapping so a typical frame (poster/backdrop or trailer) is visible with detail; no large area at pure white (255,255,255). In Show mode it glows; in Lights-up mode it reads as a screen, not a white wall. Bloom threshold set so only highlights bloom.
5. **Atmosphere:** subtle height/volumetric-style fog (cheap), faint projector cone and a few dust particles on mid/high tier, soft light cones from ceiling lights, gradient behind the screen, floor reflections only on high tier. All gated by a quality tier (Low / Medium / High / Auto) with `PerformanceMonitor`; Low must still look decent (good colours and lighting, no expensive effects).
6. **Wayfinding in 3D:** replace the big circled row badges with **small, clean row letters** placed at the aisle ends, readable from the aisle and fading with distance; hide them in Seated mode; seat numbers only for hovered/selected seats.
7. **Camera polish:** default overview angle that shows the screen and the seats attractively, depth of field off by default, FOV defaults that don't distort (Overview 50–55°, Walk 70°, Seated 60°), near-plane tuned to avoid clipping the seat you sit in, smooth easing.
8. **Performance budgets:** keep ≥ 60 FPS desktop (mid GPU) and ≥ 30 FPS on a mid-range phone at the Auto tier; report `renderer.info` (draw calls, triangles) and frame times before/after for each tier. If an effect exceeds budget, lower its quality instead of removing the whole feature. Keep using the optimised web GLB and LOD from Phase 2.

## Part D — HUD and layout cleanup (follow `DESIGN_SPEC.md`)
- **First-person modes (Walk/Seated) are minimal:** auto-hide the left and right panels (restore with a key or the toolbar); keep only crosshair, small control hints, the seat label/tooltip, mode switch, hold timer, selected-seats bar with total and Continue. Panels reappear in Overview.
- **Layout rules:** every overlay respects the fixed header height (no panel under the navbar), consistent 16 px gutters, safe-area insets on mobile, max one expanded panel at a time on small screens, no overlapping floating buttons (voice button, quality menu, mode toggle live in one toolbar cluster), panel content scrolls inside the panel, nothing cut off at 1366×768, 1920×1080 and a 390×844 phone in portrait and landscape.
- **Remove dev clutter from production:** FPS/GPU/CPU readouts, "Dev Tools", seat proxy toggles and debug logs only with `?debug=1` in dev builds. The browser extension overlay in the screenshot is not ours; ignore it.
- **Seat info panel copy:** replace the confusing disclaimer with clear wording ("Viewing angles are geometric estimates. Heads of other people are not modelled." + a "How is this calculated?" popover). Orientation note must read "Seat 01 is on your left when facing the screen." Remove the stale text.
- **Tooltips and labels:** one compact tooltip (seat, category, price, status, "Sit down (E)"), no overlapping with the crosshair; the selected-seats bar wraps cleanly with up to 8 chips and a clear "Clear" action; the total includes only what the server quoted (no client-computed totals).
- Typography and spacing from the design tokens; no truncated numbers; contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
