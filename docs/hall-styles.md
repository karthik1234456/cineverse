# CineVerse Theatre Architectural Styles & Lighting Specification
**Phase: Theatre Architecture & High-Fidelity Atmosphere**

---

## 1. Architectural Design Philosophy
CineVerse auditoriums are modeled after genuine world-class motion picture theatres, combining acoustic physics, architectural aesthetics, and SMPTE projection sightlines. Each auditorium features a distinct identity rooted in its theatre classification.

## 4. Wall & Projection Booth Systems

### 4.1 Acoustic Wall Panels & Diffuser Slats
- **Diffuser Geometry**: Vertical slats ($0.06\text{ m} \times 0.12\text{ m}$) spaced at mathematical quadratic residue diffuser (QRD) intervals along both side walls to eliminate flutter echo and diffuse side reflections.
- **Fabric Panels**: PBR textured fabric with bevelled aluminum perimeter trims.
- **Skirting Lights**: Continuous linear warm LED light channel ($0.04\text{ m}$ height) running along the baseboard skirting of both walls ($2400\text{ K}$).
- **Exit Doors & Signs**: Fully framed fire exit door portals ($1.4\text{ m} \times 2.8\text{ m}$) with illuminated green international EXIT boxes ($520\text{ nm}$ ISO 7010).

### 4.2 Rear Projection Booth & Volumetric Projector Beam
- **Booth Geometry**: Architectural housing located at the rear wall ($z = z_{\text{back}}$) elevated between $y = y_{\text{top\_tier}} + 3.2\text{ m}$ and $y = y_{\text{top\_tier}} + 5.8\text{ m}$.
- **Window Apertures**:
  - Center: Optical double-pane projector port window ($1.4\text{ m} \times 0.9\text{ m}$) with dark anti-reflective frame.
  - Flanking: Dual viewing ports ($0.9\text{ m} \times 0.7\text{ m}$) for projectionist monitor checks.
  - Interior Glow: Soft ambient monitor and equipment glow radiating through the booth glass.
- **Volumetric Projector Light Beam**:
  - **In "Lights on" mode**: Beam is dormant / near invisible ($< 0.01$ opacity).
  - **In "Show mode"**: A distinct, subtle volumetric light pyramid emanates from the projector window aperture and traverses the auditorium volume, expanding to illuminate the cinema screen proscenium ($12\%$ additive blending, soft falloff, non-distracting atmospheric realism).

## 5. Lighting Engineering & Calibration

### 5.1 Mode Specifications

| Parameter | Lights On (Default - Normal) | Lights On (Brighter Setting) | Show Mode (Cinematic) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Atmospheric Ambient Light** | $1.30$ | $1.75$ ($+35\%$) | $0.18$ |
| **Hemisphere Sky/Ground Light** | $1.50$ (warm ceiling/rich floor) | $2.00$ ($+33\%$) | $0.28$ |
| **Key Recessed Downlights** | $2.20$ ($2800\text{ K}$ warm white) | $2.95$ ($+34\%$) | $0.05$ |
| **Wall Washer Sconces** | $1.30$ ($2700\text{ K}$) | $1.75$ ($+35\%$) | $0.10$ |
| **Continuous Cove Strips** | Emissive $2.80$ | Emissive $3.60$ | Emissive $0.50$ |
| **Skirting Baseboard Lights** | Emissive $1.80$ | Emissive $2.40$ | Emissive $0.35$ |
| **Aisle Step Lights** | $0.85$ | $1.15$ | $0.40$ (safety pathway) |
| **Screen Proscenium Spill** | $0.40$ (subtle) | $0.50$ | $2.80$ (primary focal source) |
| **Projector Beam Opacity** | $0.00$ | $0.00$ | $0.12$ (additive volumetric) |

### 5.2 Luminance Verification Criteria (Lights On Mode)
- **Mean Luminance Range**: $55\text{ to }125$ (out of $255$ grayscale luminance $Y = 0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B$).
- **Near-Black Threshold**: Less than $5.0\%$ of total frame pixels with $Y < 12$.
- **Highlight Clipping Threshold**: Less than $2.0\%$ of non-screen pixels with $Y > 245$.
- **Color Distinguishability**: Seat fabrics, acoustic panels, wooden slats, and carpet runner must exhibit clear chrominance separation $(\Delta E > 15)$.
