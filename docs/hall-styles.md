# CineVerse Theatre Hall Interior Styles & Palette Architecture

## Overview
CineVerse auditorium environments have transitioned away from uniform crimson/all-red schemes to realistic, architecturally sophisticated material palettes. Walls feature neutral charcoal, warm grey acoustic slats, or cream fabrics, with dark grey and navy carpets. Saturated colors are reserved exclusively for seat upholstery categorization and elegant decorative lighting accents.

## Visible Light Fixtures & Illuminance Standards
1. **Recessed Ceiling Downlights**: Direct warm white illumination focused on seating blocks and aisle ways.
2. **Perimeter Cove Strips**: Indirect linear glow tracing ceiling perimeters and proscenium frames.
3. **Wall Acoustic Sconces**: Architectural wall fixtures providing decorative lateral illumination.
4. **Low Step Lights**: Recessed aisle riser fixtures ($< 0.1\text{m}$) maintaining safe wayfinding.
5. **Illuminance Calibration**:
   - Mean scene luminance in "Lights On" mode: $0.35 - 0.55$.
   - $\ge 60\%$ of visible pixels $> 0.15$ luminance.
   - $\le 5\%$ near-black pixels.
   - Red channel dominance capped to $\le 40\%$ across all standard views.
